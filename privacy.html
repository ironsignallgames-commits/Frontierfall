Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Frontierfall is currently in pre-release development. This policy describes the current website and test-build setup and will be reviewed before public store launch, especially if analytics, advertising, cloud accounts, purchases or additional online services are introduced.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Frontierfall website operated under the Iron Signal Games name and to the current pre-release Frontierfall game builds.

2. Website information

The Frontierfall website does not currently use its own advertising trackers, analytics scripts, account registration forms or marketing cookies.

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, the hosting service may process standard technical request information required to deliver the website, such as IP address, browser information and request logs, subject to the provider's own terms and privacy practices.

3. Game data in current test builds

Current pre-release builds primarily store game progress, characters, inventory and settings locally on the player's device.

When players use direct co-op features, limited multiplayer information needed for the session may be exchanged with the other players in that direct session. This can include a Ranger display name or Frontier ID, session status and gameplay state required for co-op.

4. External services and links

The website links to third-party services including Discord, X, Facebook and YouTube. If you follow those links, those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

When Frontierfall is released through Google Play or other stores, platform services used for downloading, updating, payments or related store functionality will also be subject to the relevant platform provider's policies.

5. Analytics, advertising and purchases

The public website does not currently run Frontierfall-owned advertising or analytics tracking. The current privacy wording does not assume future analytics, ad networks or in-app purchasing systems are active.

If any of these services are enabled, this policy will be updated to explain what information is processed, why it is used and which providers are involved.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is only expected to be shared where necessary to provide a service, comply with legal obligations, protect users or operate a third-party platform feature that the user chooses to access.

7. Your choices and requests

If you have a privacy question, believe information has been handled incorrectly, or want to ask about data associated with Frontierfall, use the Frontierfall Support page to contact us.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may change as Frontierfall moves from testing toward public release. The updated date at the top of this page will change when material revisions are published.
